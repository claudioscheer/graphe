# Graphe

The language of a Bible study: the text on screen, and the references that connect one passage or article to another.

## Language

**Dictionary cross-reference**:
A named jump from one dictionary topic to another topic in the dictionary.
_Avoid_: See-also, dict link

**Strong's link**:
A dictionary cross-reference whose topic is a Strong's number.
_Avoid_: S-tag, strong anchor

**Plain-text reference**:
A Bible reference written as words in a module, with no link tag around it.
_Avoid_: Auto-link, bare reference

**Numbered sense**:
A labeled step in a dictionary definition, such as 1, 1a, or 1a1.
_Avoid_: Sub-entry, definition item
